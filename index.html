Data Estate Ontology

The Data Estate Ontology describes a data estate: the repositories a person or small organisation keeps, the documents in them, and the control plane that governs them. It was built for one working estate, and is published so that others can describe theirs the same way.

Release 1.0, issued 27 October 2026. The estate profile follows in a later release.

What resolves here

https://w3id.org/data-estate/

docs: the overview of the model, in plain language.
vocab: classes and properties, as HTML, Turtle and JSON-LD.
scheme: the concept schemes, with every concept.
shapes: the rules an estate is checked against, as SHACL.
queries: the competency questions, as SPARQL.
profile: later, the shape of one estate in banded counts, never its contents.

Built on open standards

DCAT, PROV-O, SKOS, DPROD, schema.org, the W3C Organization Ontology and Dublin Core, with SHACL for the rules.
