Data Estate Ontology

A vocabulary for describing a data estate, and an aggregate profile of one estate described with it.

This page holds the place until the first release, due in November 2026.

What will resolve here

The namespace below is requested at w3id.org, and resolves once the request is merged.

https://w3id.org/data-estate/

vocab
Classes and properties: a data estate’s repositories as data products, and the decisions, rulings and jobs that govern them.
shapes
The rules the estate is checked against, as SHACL shapes.
scheme
Concept schemes for type, status, tier and domain.
queries
The competency questions, as SPARQL.
docs
A documentation page, and an overview of the model in plain language.
profile
Later: counts and flows by class, domain and tier. It describes the shape of an estate, never its contents.

Built on open standards

DCAT, PROV-O, SKOS, DPROD, schema.org, the W3C Organization Ontology and Dublin Core, with SHACL for the rules, and VoID and the RDF Data Cube for the profile.
