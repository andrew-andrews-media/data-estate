Data Estate Ontology: the vocabulary

The Data Estate Ontology describes a data estate: the repositories a person or small organisation keeps, the documents in them, and the control plane that governs them. It was built for one working estate, and is published so that others can describe theirs the same way.

In these definitions, a matter is an action or question the owner owes; a rulings sheet is a dated list of questions put to the owner; the decision board is where the owner answers matters and rulings, each answer filed back into the estate; the register is the estate's list of its repositories, and the entity register its list of the entities documents may name; a metadata block is the short block of keys at the head of a document; the catalogue is the index of every artefact, built from those blocks and from where files sit; and a lineage file is a repository's own record of its significant changes. Every organisation, community, event, framework, topic, place and person in the entity register is also a skos:Concept in the estate's own entity scheme, which a publication does not include: its concepts are names.

Domains and ranges are stated with schema:domainIncludes and schema:rangeIncludes, so nothing is inferred from them, and every subclass link is stated outright: no reasoner is needed.

Reused terms

These terms are reused from open standards, with their own definitions. The notes say only how this model applies them.

TermHow this model uses it
dcterms:dateAn artefact's own date, not the day it was filed; when a lineage entry's change was made; a decision's date; for a ruling from the decision board, when the owner answered.
dcterms:isPartOfThe repository that holds an artefact.
dcterms:publisherThe organisation or community that publishes a framework.
dcterms:replacesThe earlier version an artefact supersedes; an earlier answer to the same matter that a ruling replaces.
dcterms:subjectAn entity an artefact concerns.
dcterms:titleA repository's name; an artefact's title; a decision's or ruling's first bold phrase, else its first sentence; what a matter or deliverable asks to be done, answered or delivered.
dprod:DataProductEvery non-sensitive repository is also a data product.
dprod:dataProductOwnerWho owns a data product: its repository's tenant.
dprod:inputDatasetA repository a data product draws on.
dprod:purposeWhat a non-sensitive repository is for, as a data product: its register label.
org:subOrganizationOfAn entity is a subsidiary, unit, region or community of the other.
prov:wasDerivedFromThe canonical copy that a copy of an artefact derives from.
schema:EventAn event that documents concern.
schema:OrganizationAn organisation that documents concern.
schema:PersonA person that documents concern. People are names only, and are never related to another entity.
schema:PlaceA place that documents concern.
schema:mentionsAn entity that a matter's source names.
schema:organizerThe organisation or community that organises an event.
skos:altLabelAnother name an entity goes by.
skos:notationAn entity's slug, or a concept's code: a short key that never changes.
skos:prefLabelAn entity's or concept's name.
