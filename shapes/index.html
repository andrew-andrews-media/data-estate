Rules

SHACL shapes that check a data estate described with the Data Estate Ontology. Validate the estate graph merged with the vocabulary, whose concepts and subclass links the rules read. A result is a finding: the build reports it, and nothing is fixed automatically.

Namespace
https://w3id.org/data-estate/shapes#
Formats
Turtle
Licence
CC BY 4.0

A rule is a SHACL shape. Its message is what the build reports for each artefact, repository, entry or job that breaks it. Helper shapes, one for each scheme, test that a value is a concept of that scheme.

The lineage chain

The two repository rules apply to non-sensitive repositories at T1 or above, or that keep a project's record. An unmanaged T0 folder that serves no project is not checked.

RepositoryHasLineageFile

des:RepositoryHasLineageFile

A non-sensitive repository at T1 or above, or that keeps a project's record, keeps a lineage file at its root.

Applies to
de:NonSensitiveRepository
Message
“It is at T1 or above, or keeps a project's record, and has no lineage file.”

RepositoryHasLineageEntry

des:RepositoryHasLineageEntry

A non-sensitive repository at T1 or above, or that keeps a project's record, has at least one lineage entry about it.

Applies to
de:NonSensitiveRepository
Message
“It is at T1 or above, or keeps a project's record, and has no lineage entry.”

LineageEvidenceResolves

des:LineageEvidenceResolves

Every evidence path a lineage entry names exists.

Applies to
de:LineageEntry
Message
“The evidence it names cannot be found.”

ProjectHasRepository

des:ProjectHasRepository

A project has a repository, or a stated reason for having none.

Applies to
de:Project
Message
“The project has no repository, and no reason is given.”

Job records

These apply to checked jobs: those staged after results began to record the files they wrote.

JobResultStatus

des:JobResultStatus

The result's status is complete, partial, aborted, dry-run or refused. A job with no result yet is pending.

Applies to
de:CheckedJob
Message
“The job's result does not end as complete, partial, aborted, dry-run or refused.”

JobRecordsWrites

des:JobRecordsWrites

A finished job's result records the files it wrote (de:recordsWrites).

Applies to
de:CheckedJob
Message
“The job's result does not record the files it wrote.”

Value lists

Every value-list property holds a concept of its own scheme.

ValidValue

des:ValidValue

Every value of the tiers and of the nine control-plane lists is a concept of its own scheme. Artefact types, statuses and domains are checked by ValidType, ValidStatus and ValidDomain.

Applies to
de:changeType, de:deliverableState, de:domainSource, de:effect, de:jobStatus, de:matterKind, de:matterState, de:metadataSource, de:rulingState, de:tierNow, de:tierTarget
Messages
“The tier now is not T0, T1, T2 or T3.”
“The target tier is not T0, T1, T2 or T3.”
“The domain source is not declared or inherited.”
“The metadata source is not declared, inferred or path-only.”
“The change type is not lifecycle, classification, rules, deliverable-issued, cross-repo-decision, dependency, content, control-added or source-filed.”
“The effect is not close, rule, park or commit.”
“The ruling state is not open, answered, applied, carried or superseded.”
“The job status is not complete, partial, aborted, pending, dry-run or refused.”
“The matter kind is not action, ruling or go-ahead.”
“The matter state is not open, parked, answered or closed.”
“The deliverable state is not open, delivered or dropped.”
