Concept schemes

13 schemes hold the vocabulary's closed value lists. Every concept carries a label, a notation and a definition.

The word in a metadata block maps straight to its concept: type: brief becomes https://w3id.org/data-estate/type/brief. Two more schemes, of entities and tenants, stay in each estate's private graph, because their concepts are names.

The kinds of artefact a metadata block can declare.

Tiers

https://w3id.org/data-estate/scheme/tier

How managed a repository is. Tiers are cumulative: each requires everything the tier below it does. A repository has a tier now and a tier it is meant to reach.

Values of de:tierNow, de:tierTarget.

NotationLabelDefinition
T0UnmanagedA folder of files, recorded as existing and no more.
T1RegisteredRegistered with a known owner and purpose; kept in the standard layout, with a README and a decision log. Version control comes at T2.
T2VersionedAlso under version control, with a remote copy, so every change can be reviewed and reverted. No credential may ever have entered its history, and confidential client material is either absent or the remote is private.
T3RuntimeAlso runs something: a service, a timer or a container. It needs pinned dependencies, a way to deploy and a stated backup position.

Domains

https://w3id.org/data-estate/scheme/domain

The domains of work in one estate. The scheme is that estate's own: another estate would publish its own scheme, use de:domain with it unchanged, and point the rule des:DomainConcept at it. Seven domains are named, each defined by its field alone. The private ones are published as a single concept, Withheld, with nothing under it.

Values of de:domain.

NotationLabelDefinition
communityCommunity and mentoringProfessional communities and associations, and mentoring.
broadcastingBroadcastingRadio and other media.
venturesVenturesNew ventures and commercial work.
enterprise-dataEnterprise data governanceEnterprise data management and governance, as a discipline.
advocacyAdvocacy and thought leadershipSpeaking, writing and representing a field.
knowledgeKnowledge and automationHow the estate itself is run: its standards, catalogue and automation.
industry-partnershipsIndustry partnershipsJoint programmes with industry bodies.
withheldWithheldThe estate's private domains. The publication neither names them nor counts what they hold.
